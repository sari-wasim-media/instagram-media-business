## Provisional content taxonomy

The examples suggest four historical content functions:

1. Attention through memes and price comparisons.
2. Retention through sneaker information and visual curiosity.
3. Identity and aspiration through collections and celebrity style.
4. Revenue through promotions designed to match the niche's visual language.

Actual performance conclusions require post-level Insights and dates. Claude should not infer winners solely from this ten-file sample.
