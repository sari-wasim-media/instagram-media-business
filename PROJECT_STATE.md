### The 48-post history

A 48-post, 6-week experiment (4 posts/week/account) was researched, methodologically corrected across four
generations, balanced, patched and fully locked. Then, **before Post #1**, the founders decided on one feed
post per day per account. Every count, allocation, threshold and analysis window in that design is sized to 4
posts/week/account, so the execution matrix does not transfer.

The **methodology** largely does. See `experiment/methodology/CADENCE_DEPENDENCY_MAP.md` for exactly what
carries forward and what must be re-derived.

### Methodology that remains active

Both accounts tested concurrently · faceless content system · original-content-first · **zero reposts during
the controlled experiment** · no paid promotion, giveaways, Collabs or advertiser outreach · weekly Account
Status checks · **Views separated from Reach, with Reach as the denominator for every rate metric** · medians
as the principal decision statistic · T+7 feed-post snapshots and T+14 Reel tail snapshots · viral outliers
recorded as breakout/ceiling evidence only · **no performance-driven mid-test changes** · matrix frozen once
started · design isolates account effect, format effect and content-engine effect.

Full detail: `experiment/methodology/MEASUREMENT_AND_RULES.md`.

### Originality standard — stated precisely

**Zero unoriginal/reposted feed posts during the controlled experiment.** This is an experiment and business
policy decision, adopted for clean attribution, to avoid wasting matrix slots on suppressed posts, to test the
team's ability to produce original content at cadence, to remove interpretive ambiguity, and as a margin of
safety.

⚠ **It is not true that one repost makes an account globally recommendation-ineligible for 30 days.** Keep the
two mechanisms separate: **account-level** eligibility turns on repetition (10+ unoriginal posts in 30 days),
and its recovery mechanism applies only to accounts that already crossed that threshold — a clean account has
no clock to reset. **Post-level** treatment is independent and judges the file, not the account. An earlier
project document states this incorrectly; see `strategy/historical/CORRECTIONS_LEDGER.md`.
