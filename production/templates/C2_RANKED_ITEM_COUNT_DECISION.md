# C2 ranked-item count — proposed template-lock value

**Status: PROPOSED — FOUNDER REVIEW REQUIRED.** This is the one genuine non-matrix lever the Sourcing Gate
names (`GATES.md` §1.1), and the lock specification requires it to be fixed at template lock
(`LOCK_SPECIFICATION.md` §1 item 17). Once the founders confirm a value it is frozen for all 21 C2 posts in
every format.

## Recommendation

> **Fix the C2 ranked-item count at 7 — in every C2 piece, in every format, for all six weeks.**

Runner-up: 8. Rejected: 5 and 6 (thin), 9 and 10 (impossible inside the locked carousel band with a hook and a
summary slide).

---

## Why the count has to be fixed at all

Two locked reasons. It controls the image-rights burden — every ranked item needs one rights-clean image
per piece in every format, so 21 × N distinct images. And a drifting count would be an uncontrolled exposure
variable inside C2: a 5-item Reel and a 10-item Reel differ in duration, density and swipe depth, which
would confound the engine read with a template read.

## The constraints the value has to satisfy

| Constraint | Source | Effect on N |
|---|---|---|
| Carousel 5–10 slides | locked (`GATES.md` §4) | Hook + N items + summary ≤ 10 → **N ≤ 8** |
| Reel 20–59 s, target 30–45 s | locked | At a legible 4.0 s per item plus 3.0 s hook, 0.4 s per rank reveal, 4.0 s summary, 1.5 s end card: N = 5 → 29 s · 6 → 33 s · **7 → 36.5 s** · 8 → 40 s |
| One rights-clean image per item, per piece, never reused | locked (anti-duplication rule 4) | 21 × N images: 5 → 105 · 6 → 126 · **7 → 147** · 8 → 168 |
| The same ranking must also ship as a single Static | locked format mix | N items must be legible on one 1080 × 1350 image |
| Content-quality minimum | locked | The list must be worth saving |

## The argument for 7

1. **It is the largest count that keeps one spare slide.** Hook + 7 + summary = 9. The tenth slide can hold
   the "how we ranked" method slide when a ranking basis needs a sentence — which is exactly where the
   content-quality minimum's "every factual claim verified against a named source" gets shown to the
   audience. At 8 there is no room for it; at 6 the room is wasted.
2. **It centres the Reel.** 36.5 s sits in the middle of the 30–45 s target at a per-item dwell (4.0 s) that
   a reader can actually use. Five items only reach 30 s by padding each item to 5 s, which slows the Reel
   to fill time.
3. **It reads as a guide, not a list.** A seven-item summary slide is a reference card people keep; five is a
   list people glance at. Saves per reach is C2's key metric.
4. **The static composes.** A hero #1 above a 2 × 3 grid makes the top pick visually dominant — the editorial
   point of a ranking — and every thumbnail stays above 290 px wide. Eight forces a 2 × 4 grid of equals or an
   awkward two-hero layout; six flattens the hierarchy.
5. **It is a real, not token, saving on rights.** 147 images instead of 168 is 21 fewer sourcing-and-
   provenance cycles, one per piece, without touching the content's authority. Five would save 63 images but
   at the cost of the format's reason to exist.
6. **Odd counts count down better.** 7 → 1 gives three beats of build before the podium (3, 2, 1) and a
   natural mid-point at 4; even counts split flat.

## What the founders give up by choosing otherwise

- **Choose 8** and you gain one item of authority per list at the cost of 21 more images, the method slide,
  and a denser static; the Reel runs 40.6 s, still in band.
- **Choose 6** and you save 21 images and ~9 minutes a piece; the Reel sits at 33 s, the static becomes a
  2 × 3 grid with no hero, and lists read a touch thin.
- **Choose 5** and the sourcing burden drops to 105 images, but the format stops being a guide, the Reel needs
  padding to reach 30 s, and C2's key metric (saves) is the one most exposed to thinness. Not recommended.

## Consequences once locked at 7 (all handled in the templates)

- Every C2 hook states the count: "7 …", "Every … ranked" is only used when the set genuinely has 7 members.
- Carousel: slide 1 hook · slides 2–8 items **counting down 7 → 1** · slide 9 summary listing 1 → 7 · optional
  slide 10 method (only when needed; still 7 items).
- Reel: 36.5 s at house pacing; cover = hook frame.
- Static: hero #1 + 2 × 3 grid, all 7 images present.
- Tier-list variant: the same 7 items placed into ≤ 4 tiers (S / A / B / C); item slides carry a tier badge
  instead of a rank number; the summary is the tier board. Still 7 items, still 7 images.
- Sourcing Gate bank: **42 rights-clean C2 images** cover Week 1's four C2 pieces and the two C2 reserve
  pieces; the whole experiment needs 147.
- Sustainability test (`../assets/C2_SUSTAINABILITY_TEST.md`): two pieces at 7 items each, inside the declared
  budgets in `C2_RANKINGS_AND_TIER_LISTS.md` §8.
