# Historical 48-Post Design — SUPERSEDED BEFORE LAUNCH

# ⚠ THIS IS NOT THE ACTIVE PLAN. DO NOT EXECUTE ANYTHING IN THIS DIRECTORY.

The 48-post revival experiment was fully designed, corrected, balanced and methodologically locked. **Then,
before a single post was published, the founders superseded it** by deciding on one feed post per day per
account — 7/week/account, 14 combined/week, 6 posting weeks, **84 planned feed posts total**.

**No revival-experiment post was ever published under this design.**

**The active, founder-locked 84-post design is in `../active-84-post-plan/`** — the matrix itself is
`../active-84-post-plan/matrix_84.csv`. What survives methodologically is in `../methodology/`.

These documents are preserved because the **methodology** is valuable and much of it carries forward. The
**execution matrix** does not.

---

## Why it was superseded

Not because it was wrong. It was superseded because the founders changed the cadence.

At 4 feed posts/week/account the design produced 24 posts per account and 48 total, and **every count,
allocation, threshold and analysis window in these documents is sized to that number.** At 7/week/account the
totals change to 42 per account and 84 total, so the matrix, the per-cell allocations, the slot
counterbalancing and the sample sizes must all be re-derived.

`../methodology/CADENCE_DEPENDENCY_MAP.md` records exactly what transfers and what must be rebuilt.

## The three documents, in order

They correct each other. **Later beats earlier.**

### 1. `REVIVAL_EXPERIMENT_FINAL_LOCK.md` — Generation 3

The first full lock: content cells C1–C4, measurement framework, threshold table, weekly review protocol,
the Scenario A–H decision tree, and the 26-item Week 0 checklist.

⚠ **Four things in this document were later withdrawn. Do not quote it on any of them:**

- **"One unoriginal post resets the 30-day clock and invalidates the eligibility read"** (§2, §7, §13) —
  **withdrawn by Generation 4.** This is the single most important correction in the project. Account-level
  eligibility turns on repetition (10+ in 30 days) and recovery applies only to accounts that already crossed
  that threshold; post-level treatment is separate and judges the file, not the account.
- **The "living-follower rate" metric and the 2.3% / 3.7% liveness figures** (§1, §4, §9, §10) — **withdrawn
  by Generation 4** as a non-follower artefact. Stories are a supporting diagnostic and gate nothing.
- **The per-cell format allocation** (§5) — **replaced by Generation 4**; it did not control format.
- **Account-welded posting slots** (§11) — **replaced by Generation 4** with counterbalanced slots.

Still valid and carried forward: the C1–C4 cell definitions, the Views-vs-Reach discipline, T+7 / T+14
snapshots, medians as the decision statistic, the frozen-variable list, and the legacy-cleanup criteria.

**The Week 0 checklist in §15 belongs to this superseded design.** Its completion status — including which
items a later decision superseded — is tracked in `../../evidence/WEEK_0_CHECKLIST_STATUS.md`. Do not read it
as a live to-do list.

### 2. `PATCH_AND_FREEZE_48_POST_MATRIX.md` — Generation 4

Corrections 1–4 and **the complete balanced 48-post matrix** (§2), with per-account row tables, balance
verification, and the analysis of how each effect is estimated. This is the most methodologically mature
document in the project and the best model for how the 84-post matrix should be built.

⚠ **One section of it was deleted by Generation 5: §6.** That section still contains the Week 4 gate, the
"double down" rule, the "drop a cell" rule and the weeks 5–6 adaptation window. **All of it was removed.**
Read §6 only as history.

### 3. `FINAL_METHODOLOGY_PATCH_MATRIX_FROZEN.md` — Generation 5

The final word on the 48-post design. Removes **all** performance-based adaptation, freezes all six weeks,
converts Week 4 into a diagnostic/safety/data-quality checkpoint with no allocation authority, and restates
the early-stop, disruption and protocol-violation rules.

**Where this document and Generation 4 disagree, this one wins.**

Its own closing gate — *"REVIVAL EXPERIMENT FULLY LOCKED — PROCEED TO WEEK 0"* — applied to the 48-post design
at that time. **It does not authorise launch today**, and it refers to a matrix that is no longer active. The
active matrix is the founder-locked 84-post design in `../active-84-post-plan/`.

**Provenance note:** this file is a **reconstruction**, not a byte-exact export. Per the supplemental
package's own README it was rebuilt from the founder-supplied final methodology-patch transcript, with a
supersession warning appended. Faithful in substance; not a verbatim original.

## Preserved warning about production load

Generation 3's closing gate named the binding risk plainly: *"48 original pieces in six weeks from a team that
has never produced original content at volume."* Its Week 0 item 23 set an explicit go/no-go — if week 1's
pieces are not produced and scheduled before week 1 begins, cut cadence rather than start and slip.

The active locked design raises that load from 48 pieces to 84, and week 1 from 8 pieces to 14 — plus 84
Stories. The warning survives as the two binding launch gates in `../active-84-post-plan/GATES.md`.

**This is recorded as a preserved analyst warning alongside a founder decision that postdates it.** The
cadence decision is the founders' to make and stands. The warning is kept because it was the design's own
stated largest risk and should inform the 84-post production plan.
