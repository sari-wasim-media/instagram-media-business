# Caption, hashtag and alt-text policy — one policy, both accounts, all 84 posts

**Status: PROPOSED — founder confirmation required.** The hashtag policy is a held-fixed production
variable already locked in outline (`LOCK_SPECIFICATION.md` §1 item 17: *3–5 topically specific tags in the
caption, no follow-bait or generic reach tags*). This document gives it the exact operating form, and fixes
the caption architecture and the alt-text rule alongside it. Platform fact that binds: Instagram now
allows **at most 5 tags on a post** — a sixth stops the caption/comment posting.

## 1. Caption architecture — every post, in this order

1. **Hook line** — ≤ 100 characters, mirrors the on-screen hook; the only line most readers see. At most one
   emoji, never at the start.
2. **Body** — 2–5 short lines. C1: the four facts and the verdict. C2: the full list, 1 → 7, one value each.
   C3: metric, basis, window, "Data as of", takeaway, "Snapshot, not advice." C4: the line or one extra beat.
3. **Source line** — C1 and C3 always; C2 when any chip carries a number: `Source: <name>, <date>`.
4. **AI disclosure line** — the six AI slots only, verbatim from `AI_IMAGERY_POLICY_PACK.md` §3.
5. **Hashtags** — 3–5, on their own line at the end, in the caption (never in a first comment, so the
   treatment is identical across all 84 posts).

Nothing else: no "link in bio" (locked: no link), no "DM us", no retailer links or codes, no calls for
likes, follows, saves, comments or tags, no "part 2 coming", no giveaways, no @-mentions of brands, stores
or people (a mention is an outreach signal and a variable), no location tag.

Voice: the desk's — declarative, specific, sentence case, no exclamation marks, British or American spelling
chosen once and kept (proposed: **American**, matching the audience's largest country).

## 2. Hashtag policy — the operating form

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Count | **3, 4 or 5.** Never fewer, never more (platform hard cap 5) |
| Placement | In the caption, last line, both accounts, all 84 posts |
| Composition | **2–3 subject tags** (brand, model, colorway as commonly written: `#airjordan1`, `#newbalance990`, `#asicsgel`) + **1 engine/category tag** from the fixed list below + **0–1 community tag** from the fixed list |
| Fixed category tags | C1 `#sneakerrelease` · C2 `#sneakerranking` · C3 `#sneakerresale` (alt `#sneakermarket`) · C4 `#sneakermemes` |
| Fixed community tags (at most one) | `#sneakers` · `#sneakerhead` · `#streetwear` |
| Banned | `#fyp` `#foryou` `#viral` `#explore` `#explorepage` `#trending` `#followme` `#follow4follow` `#f4f` `#like4like` `#l4l` `#likeforlike` `#instagood` `#photooftheday` `#reels` `#reelsinstagram` `#sneakersforsale` `#cop` `#reps` `#ua` and any replica-adjacent, giveaway, growth-service or unrelated tag |
| Never | Tags of a specific retailer, store or marketplace; a tag naming a real person; a tag on a Story |

Subject tags are the keyword-relevance mechanism C1 relies on and are the only part that varies per post.

## 3. Alt text — every image, every slide, every Reel cover

One descriptive sentence in the alt-text field, written for a person who cannot see the image: what the
product is, what the slide says. Template per engine in the template specs. No hashtags, no keyword
stuffing, no "image of". Written at production, checked at QA. Identical practice on both accounts.

## 4. Length and legibility

Captions ≤ 900 characters including tags (well inside the platform limit, above the fold for the hook).
Line breaks between sections. Numbers written as they appear on the artwork.
