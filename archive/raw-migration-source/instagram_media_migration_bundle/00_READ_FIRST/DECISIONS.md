## Platform/content policy decisions
- Historical repost-heavy operating model is not the future model.

- Targeted replica/UA cleanup only; three identified UAPLG posts were removed after documentation.
- `sneakerbong` username is not being changed before the revival evidence exists.

## Experiment decisions
- Both accounts are tested; no winner chosen in advance.
- Six posting weeks plus measurement tail remains the working duration.
- The old 48-post matrix is ARCHIVED/SUPERSEDED, not active.
- Active cadence decision: 1 feed post/day/account, 84 feed posts total over 6 weeks.
- No performance-based matrix adaptation mid-test.
- No extra feed post triggered solely by virality during the controlled test.
- No paid promotion, giveaway, Collab, or advertiser outreach during initial revival measurement.

## Week 0 profile freeze
The profiles were cleaned/setup before the test. During the controlled experiment, do not casually change username, bio, profile picture, pins/highlights, link, or other profile variables in response to performance.
