# Asset-class decision — which source classes supply each engine, in priority order

**Sourcing Gate item 2 — PASSED 2026-09-08.** The priority order is **ACTIVE — delegated production
decision (2026-09-08, C2 order revised 2026-09-13)**, re-evaluated against the trademark review; the
residual-risk acceptance in §4 is **ACTIVE — founder decision, ACCEPTED 2026-09-08**
(`../TRADEMARK_RISK_REVIEW.md`). Photographs of branded sneakers may be used under the review's §5
nominative-use operating rule, which is mandatory on every piece. Research basis:
`SOURCING_ROUTES_RESEARCH.md` (accessed 2026-09-05).

**Revision of 2026-09-13.** The founders stated that they have no meaningful access to a sneaker inventory
and cannot self-shoot products at scale. The C2 order below is re-ranked accordingly: `OWNED` moves from
first to last and becomes opportunistic; `LICENSED_FREE` becomes the primary class. **Later the same day the
founders decided not to solicit permissions from any third party, so `PERMISSIONED` is not an active route**
(it stays defined below because `GATES.md` §1.2 lists it). The
classes themselves, their evidence requirements and the exclusions are unchanged, so the gate item stays
passed and no §3 re-review trigger of the trademark review fires (no new imagery route). The C2 order is
**provisional until the two-piece stress test** (`C2_SUSTAINABILITY_TEST.md`) reports; the test, not this
document, decides whether the order is sustainable.

The acceptable classes are locked (`GATES.md` §1.2): owned · licensed · permissioned · self-generated ·
brand/retailer press assets only where terms explicitly permit editorial social use, verified per brand and
per asset. Not acceptable: scraped marketplace photography · other accounts' images · watermarked third-party
images · reposted templates. This document orders those classes per engine and names the exact licences.

## 1. The classes as the provenance register records them

| Class code | Meaning | Proof required in the provenance record |
|---|---|---|
| `SELF_GENERATED` | Charts, typography, house UI, illustration drawn by the founders | Who made it, date |
| `OWNED` | The founders' own photographs (faceless product shots of a pair that happens to be at hand — **not a scalable route**, 2026-09-13) | Who shot it, date, device |
| `LICENSED_FREE` | Unsplash License · Pexels License · CC0 · CC BY (attribution in caption; **CC BY not used in the initial stress test** — founder correction 2026-09-13) | Source URL, photographer, licence name, **archived copy of the licence text**, date, attribution text if required |
| `LICENSED_PAID` | Adobe Stock Standard (or an equivalent standard licence covering social use) — never an "editorial use only" asset | Licence reference / invoice, archived licence text, date |
| `PERMISSIONED` | A photographer's, collector's or store's written permission for this use — **not an active route: the founders do not solicit permissions (founder decision, 2026-09-13)** | The message or e-mail, archived, with date and scope |
| `PRESS_VERIFIED` | A brand press asset whose newsroom terms the founders read and archived, and which permit editorial social use | Terms URL, archived text, the clause, date read |
| `AI_GENERATED` | Six C4 slots only | Tool and version, prompt, seed/params, attestation, reviewer, disclosure, platform label — `../policy/AI_IMAGERY_POLICY_PACK.md` |

Excluded even though they look convenient: Pixabay content with visible brands (its licence bars commercial
use of such content) · CC BY-SA images (the share-alike would attach to the whole slide) · editorial-licence
stock (Shutterstock Editorial, Getty Editorial) · any brand press asset without a read-and-archived
permission · anything from another Instagram account · anything with a third-party watermark.

## 2. Priority order per engine

### C1 — Release & Verdict Desk

1. `SELF_GENERATED` — the verdict card, Reel and carousel are fully producible without a photograph (the
   no-image layout is first-class). **This is the default**, so C1 can never Miss on imagery.
2. `OWNED` — when a founder owns or can photograph the pair.
3. `LICENSED_FREE` / `LICENSED_PAID` — when the release exists in stock (rare for a new colorway; common for
   retros and general releases).
4. `PRESS_VERIFIED` — only for brands with read-and-archived permissive terms (currently PUMA's Media Hub
   states editorial clearance; Amer Sports' press material with source credit). **Nike, Jordan, adidas,
   ASICS, HOKA, Vans, Crocs press imagery is not usable** under the terms read on 2026-09-05.

### C2 — Rankings & Tier Lists (order revised 2026-09-13)

1. `LICENSED_FREE` — for the initial stress test, in this order: CC0 / public-domain-equivalent (Wikimedia
   Commons, Flickr; Openverse as a discovery aid), Pexels License, Unsplash License, then any other clearly
   licensed-free asset only where its requirements are unambiguous and met exactly; **CC BY is not used in
   the initial test** (founder correction 2026-09-13 — it stays in the class as a possible future route,
   pending a check that its attribution can be satisfied cleanly on Instagram); the licence text archived
   per asset and an **ownership-plausibility check** per candidate against licence laundering; **narrowed** (trademark review §6): only photographs in which the
   product is the subject in a neutral context; no brand advertising imagery, no store or event scenes with
   brand signage as the subject, nothing that could depict counterfeit product, no people. The licence
   excludes the *trademark* in frame, not the photograph. Covers icons and general releases; covers niche
   colorways poorly — the stress test measures how poorly. Never Unsplash+, never CC BY-SA, never Pixabay
   for a branded product.
2. `PRESS_VERIFIED` — brand by brand, as for C1 (PUMA on the archived reading; any further brand is a §3
   re-review trigger — escalate first).
3. `LICENSED_PAID` — Adobe Stock Standard for gaps, single credits, never editorial-only assets.
   **Every purchase needs founder approval** (no spending authority; none on file); a gap-filler, not a
   route. Items that would have used it are counted by the stress test (`WOULD_USE_P`).
4. `SELF_GENERATED` illustration — the colour-blocked house silhouette (`C2_SOURCING_ROUTES.md` route S).
   Always available; makes a specific colorway recognisable without any photograph. The always-on fallback,
   so no C2 slot is missed for want of a clearable photograph; it reduces the trade-dress residual rather than
   removing it (review §7). Its share per piece is reported, not hidden.
5. `OWNED` — **opportunistic only** (2026-09-13): a pair that is physically at hand is shot on the plate; no
   third-party copyright to clear, the same trademark residual as any photograph. Never a planning input;
   no inventory, no rig budget, no theme written toward pairs that are not in the room.

*Not on the active path:* `PERMISSIONED` — **the founders do not send permission requests to creators,
photographers, Instagram accounts or other third parties** (founder decision, 2026-09-13). An image usable
only with a permission nobody will ask for is rejected (`UNCLEAR_RIGHTS`), not credited. **Credit is not
permission.**

### C3 — Market & Price Intelligence

1. `SELF_GENERATED` only. Charts and data cards; no photographs. Its gate item is the data-source terms
   register, not imagery: `C3_DATA_SOURCE_TOS_REGISTER.md`.

### C4 — Original Meme & Commentary

1. `SELF_GENERATED` — house UI (receipt, ticket, chat), typography, illustration. Three of the seven formats
   need no imagery at all.
2. `OWNED` photographs.
3. `LICENSED_FREE` / `LICENSED_PAID` as for C2.
4. `AI_GENERATED` — **only** in the six locked slots, never as a fallback anywhere else.

### Stories

`SELF_GENERATED` · `OWNED` · `LICENSED_FREE`. **No AI imagery in any Story.** No reshared content.

## 4. The residual risk — ACCEPTED by the founders, 2026-09-08

Every photograph of a branded sneaker — owned, licensed or permissioned — shows a trademark and a protected
trade dress. Copyright is cleared by ownership or licence; the trademark is not "cleared" by anyone and is
handled by **nominative, editorial, non-endorsing use** under the operating rule in the review: the product
is named and shown only as needed to discuss, rank or review it; no third-party mark serves as the desk's own
identifier; no decorative logos or branded framing; nothing implies sponsorship, partnership, authorisation
or endorsement; the editorial context is obvious on every piece. The focused review — with the U.S. and
Israeli legal positions, a calibrated risk table, the rule, and the single recommendation
(**ACCEPT**, an operational risk recommendation, not a guarantee of legal immunity) — is
`../TRADEMARK_RISK_REVIEW.md`.

**The founders accepted it on 2026-09-08.** §2 applies as written, Sourcing Gate item 2 passes, and the
42-image bank build proceeds on the routes in §2 — since 2026-09-13 primarily F and X rather than O and F,
the same six routes in a different order — with the review's §5 nominative-use operating rule mandatory on
every piece and checked at QA. The review's §3 re-review triggers stand; §7 of the review records the
illustration-only fallback if one is ever hit.
