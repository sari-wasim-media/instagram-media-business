## The venture

**HISTORICAL FACT** — Sari and Wasim are equal 50/50 partners reviving two dormant sneaker/streetwear
Instagram accounts. Sari is the primary administrator of both; Wasim also has access.

**HISTORICAL FACT** — Both accounts were dormant for years before this project. `thestreetwearofficial`
remained active into late 2023; `sneakerbong` last posted in 2021.

**DEFERRED DECISION** — Long-term operating roles between the founders have not been assigned.

## Hard constraints

**FIXED CONSTRAINT — Faceless.** Neither founder will appear on camera, appear in published photographs or
video, become the public personality behind either account, or rely on founder-as-influencer positioning. The
business must work as a faceless media brand.

> **Scope note.** This constraint covers *visual appearance and public-personality positioning*. Prior
> analysis considered voiceover, text-on-screen narration and a named editorial persona to be compatible with
> it and to count as authorship — but **that is analysis, not a founder decision**. Voiceover is not
> prohibited by the constraint, and it has not been separately approved. Do not state it as settled either
> way. See `../../DECISIONS.md`.

**FIXED CONSTRAINT — No replica/counterfeit advertisers, permanently.** Including "UA" sellers. This is not
a per-deal judgement; it is a standing exclusion.

**FIXED CONSTRAINT — No reckless automation** that risks the Instagram accounts. Official
Meta/Instagram-compatible methods are the default. No grey automation, private APIs, mass-spam behaviour or
botting.

**FIXED CONSTRAINT — Content boundaries.** No sexual, political or drug-related content; no scams or
deceptive promotions; no advertisers unrelated to the audience.

## Money

**HISTORICAL FACT** — Initial private setup budget capped at **$300**. No external capital.

**HISTORICAL FACT** — Founders do not intend to withdraw profits during the first 12 months; revenue is
reinvested.

**CURRENT GOAL** — Roughly **$6,000/month** revenue. A target to be tested, not a forecast. Follower count is
explicitly secondary to economic quality.

**CURRENT GOAL, heavily qualified** — The original ambition included **500,000 combined followers** and no
more than 10% combined founder involvement. Later research judged 500K *"generous"* rather than merely
optimistic (only 21% of accounts under 10,000 followers grew at all in 2026) and warned that pursuing it
would push the business back toward the volume-reposting behaviour the platform now penalises. **Treat the
follower target as a de-emphasised aspiration, not a plan.**

## Historical operations

**HISTORICAL FACT** — Revenue came from selling placements. The 2019–2021 price menu ran $20 for a 24-hour
Story to $35 for a permanent feed post, with link-in-bio at $6/day. Preserved at
`../historical/HISTORICAL_PRICE_MENU.md` as **evidence only — not an approved price list**.

**HISTORICAL FACT** — Payments were received through PayPal. No formal invoicing, contract or record-keeping
structure was ever established.

**HISTORICAL FACT** — The historical operating model was repost-heavy. Evidence in
`../../evidence/historical-performance/` includes posts carrying third-party watermarks. **This is explicitly
not the future model.**

**HISTORICAL FACT — bounded** — At least two historical advertisers priced trademarked goods far below
authenticated-market levels. Three such promotions were identified and removed in Week 0.

> ⚠ **Do not overstate this.** An earlier claim that *the historical advertiser base operated in replicas* was
> **withdrawn as overstated** — the evidence covers two named advertisers across four creatives. That is a
> fact about those creatives, not about the advertiser base. Equally, the claim that a meaningful portion of
> the 44,800 followers followed *for* replicas was withdrawn as an untested hypothesis.

**HISTORICAL FACT** — Some historical posts reportedly reached 300,000–1,000,000 impressions. **Reported, not
evidenced**, and from a platform that distributed aggregator content very differently. It has no predictive
value and should not appear in any model.

## Commercial direction

**CURRENT GOAL** — Recurring, niche-aligned advertising rather than one-off promotions: relevant sneaker and
streetwear brands, suppliers, retailers, resale stores, e-commerce businesses.

**DEFERRED DECISION — The monetization model has not been chosen.** Direct Instagram sponsorship remains
open; a content-production/services model remains open; nothing is selected.

> An earlier research round ranked six revenue structures and favoured a productised content-service. That
> ranking was **explicitly retracted** in the next generation — *"This document contains no revenue-model
> recommendation… nothing is chosen."* Do not treat the services model as adopted.

**WORKING HYPOTHESIS** — Direct Instagram sponsorship is not structurally plausible at current scale. At a
benchmark reach rate on ~22K followers a post reaches roughly 1,300 accounts, which prices below the 2019
menu. Plausible later, not now.

**WORKING HYPOTHESIS** — A services model would be roughly linear in labour and therefore in direct tension
with the 10%-involvement goal. The founders should decide consciously whether they want a media asset or a
cash-generating service that funds one. **Unresolved and deliberately so.**

**WORKING HYPOTHESIS** — The category advertising layer may be contracting. An early claim of ~20%/year
structural collapse was **softened**: the decline rate roughly halved, management described deferral rather
than loss, and the reference company remained profitable. Now a **risk hypothesis, explicitly removed from
the experiment design**, not a proven market condition.

**FIXED CONSTRAINT — Excluded buyers**, beyond replicas: bot and proxy vendors for sneaker copping (they
advertise heavily in this niche, they will find the accounts, and they pay well), follower-growth and
engagement services, crypto/NFT-adjacent, gambling, dropship arbitrage, and anything paying above market —
that premium is a risk signal.

> The offer will arrive in a month when revenue is behind. The exclusion list exists so the answer is decided
> before the temptation.

## Growth and expansion

**HISTORICAL FACT** — Organic growth is preferred. Buying accounts must not be the foundation of the plan.

**WORKING HYPOTHESIS** — The cheap version of a page network (one content line, many pages) does not work:
near-identical detection judges the file, so posting the same asset across accounts is a negative signal
rather than multiple chances at reach. **Every page must have its own content line.**

**DEFERRED DECISION** — Formal brand architecture, expansion into additional niches, and any network build
are all post-experiment decisions.

**DEFERRED DECISION — `sneakerbong` rename.** Not renaming before revival evidence exists. No evidence was
found that Instagram penalises the name. The stronger argument for an eventual rename is whether the name
describes a media product a business would buy from — not the word "bong". Revisit at the decision point.

## Long-term

**CURRENT GOAL** — An end-to-end operating system spanning discovery, rights checks, production, approvals,
scheduling, publishing, community management, DMs, lead qualification, proposals, payments, delivery,
reporting, renewals and analytics. Authority moves to automation gradually, only after reliability is proven.

**WORKING HYPOTHESIS** — The originality requirement structurally lowers the automation ceiling, because the
thing that must be original is the hardest thing to automate. Any automation design that maximises output
volume drifts toward the behaviour that removes the accounts from recommendations.

**FIXED CONSTRAINT — Must remain manual indefinitely:** advertiser vetting (the single most important human
gate given the historical exposure), brand-safety judgement, pricing and negotiation, contract terms, anything
touching account access, and the decision of what to post when the calendar is empty.

**Nothing gets built until after the revival experiment produces its decision.**

## Governance gap

**NOT DONE** — No legal entity, no written partnership terms, no deadlock mechanism for a 50/50 split, no
contracts. Flagged in the risk register as low-detectability until a dispute occurs. Recommended to be settled
before first revenue.
