## 1. Sourcing Gate

Imagery rights are the project's binding operational constraint. The gate below is the canonical Week-0 item
("write down what your source is for each of the first 8 pieces") scaled to the current requirement of
**22 pieces** — 14 Week-1 feed posts plus the 7-piece reserve, plus one AI-route test piece.

### 1.1 Quantified burden

| Engine | Posts | Reels | Carousels | Statics | Rights exposure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | 21 | 12 | 6 | 3 | Moderate — release imagery, often only available as brand/retailer press assets |
| **C2** | **21** | **12** | **6** | **3** | **Highest.** One rights-clean image per ranked item, per piece, in every format |
| C3 | 21 | 12 | 6 | 3 | **Lowest.** Visuals are self-generated charts. Its constraint is research time and data-source ToS |
| C4 | 21 | 12 | 6 | 3 | Low–moderate. Text-led; six slots are AI-treated |

**C2 is the whole problem.** The canonical format is "slide 1 hook, slides 2–9 one item each, final slide
summary", and the Reel carries the same ranking — so **every C2 piece needs one rights-clean image per ranked
item regardless of format**. At 4–8 items per piece across 21 C2 posts, with "different source imagery for
every piece" binding, that is **roughly 85–170 distinct rights-clean product images**. Whole-experiment
estimate: **≈130–250 images**, dominated by C2.

⚠ **Correcting a bundling in older documents:** several checklists say "imagery sourcing for C2 and C3". These
are different constraints in kind. C2 is image rights at volume; **C3 is research time plus data-source terms
of service on self-generated charts.**

**The format mix is not the lever.** Reallocating C2's carousels into Reels leaves the asset count essentially
unchanged, and reducing an engine's share is prohibited. The **asset pipeline** is the lever. The one genuine
non-matrix lever is the **C2 ranked-item count**, which is a template parameter and must be fixed at template
lock — both to control the rights burden and because a drifting item count would be an uncontrolled exposure
variable inside C2.

### 1.2 Acceptable source classes

**Owned** (founders' own photography — faceless-compatible, zero rights risk, highest cost) · **licensed**
(paid stock or editorial licences covering commercial social use) · **permissioned** (explicit written
permission from a photographer, collector or creator) · **self-generated** (charts, data cards, typography,
illustration — rights-free by construction) · **brand/retailer press assets** only where the licence terms
explicitly permit editorial social use, verified per brand and per asset.

**Not acceptable:** scraped marketplace photography · other accounts' images · watermarked third-party images ·
reposted templates.

### 1.3 Gate items — all must pass

1. **Templates built and measured first.** The four house templates (ranking carousel, verdict card, chart,
   meme layout) exist, and the number of distinct assets each consumes is recorded. **The C2 ranked-item count
   is fixed here.** Without these numbers the gate cannot be evaluated.
2. **Asset-class decision recorded** for each of C1–C4: which source classes supply its imagery, in priority
   order.
3. **The bank covers 22 pieces** at the measured per-piece asset counts.
4. **Provenance record for every asset**: source, class, licence or permission reference, date, permitted use.
   No asset without a provenance record may be used in any of the 84 posts.
5. **C2 sustainability test.** Two complete C2 pieces produced end-to-end from the chosen route, with elapsed
   time and asset count recorded. If two pieces cannot be produced rights-clean inside the declared time
   budget, the route fails — the route changes, not the matrix.
6. **C3 data-source ToS check.** Per source, confirm manual collection and republication of derived figures is
   permitted. Sources recorded. **No scraper during the experiment.**
7. **C4 imagery source confirmed** as owned / licensed / self-generated, with the never-a-reposted-template
   rule in the production checklist.
8. **No gate item requires reposting, Collabs, or any relaxation of the originality rule.**

### 1.4 AI arm of the Sourcing Gate

9. **Generation tool(s) named**, and their output licence **confirmed to permit commercial use**.
   ⚠ *This is a factual verification the founders must perform. It is NOT done.*
10. **One complete AI-treated C4 piece produced end-to-end** as the AI-route sustainability test, with elapsed
    production time and compliance-review time recorded.
11. That piece verified against the full prohibited-use list in `AI_IMAGERY_SUBTEST.md` §5, carrying the fixed
    disclosure line, with any platform AI label observed and not evaded.
12. Confirmation that no AI asset reconstructs or launders a third-party image, and that no AI asset appears in
    C1, C2 or C3.

## 2. Week-1 Gate — two layers

Week 1 contains **11 non-C1 slots and 3 C1 slots**. C1 is canonically news-sensitive (reacting within 24–48 h),
so it cannot be both finished before Day 1 and genuinely responsive. The gate is therefore two-layer — without
being made any easier to pass.

### 2.1 Layer 1 — must be completely finished before Day 1

Any item missing means **do not start**.

1. **All 11 non-C1 Week-1 feed pieces** fully produced, captioned, rights-cleared, QA'd against the
   content-quality minimum, and **scheduled in-platform** at 16:00 UTC on their assigned dates.
2. All four house templates finished, asset counts measured, C2 ranked-item count fixed.
3. **Sourcing Gate passed**, including the AI arm.
4. Tracking sheet live with all 84 rows pre-populated from `matrix_84.csv`.
5. Topic backlog live, queue rule operating, C1 release-calendar watchlist populated for the Week-1 window.
6. **7-piece evergreen reserve built** (no C1 reserve; no AI-treated reserve piece).
7. All 14 Week-2 outlines complete, and the rolling-buffer rule (≥7 finished pieces banked) satisfied.
8. **The D2 C1 topic lock executed.** `A-W1-C1-CAR-01` publishes at 16:00 UTC on Day 2, so its T−48 h deadline
   falls at 16:00 UTC on **Day 0 — before launch**. It is a Layer-1 item.
9. **Story readiness**: Story template built · the **7-day rotation predeclared** · the interactive-sticker
   weekday fixed · **Week 1's 14 Stories (7 per account) produced and queued or scheduled**.
10. **AI-imagery policy pack complete**: definition, prohibited-use list, **fixed disclosure wording**,
    provenance fields, and the six locked slot IDs recorded in the tracking sheet.
11. **Week-0 audience and Account Status baseline captured** on both accounts.

*Descriptive-only, non-blocking:* the Week-0 weekly account Reach / non-follower Reach baseline. Retained
because it is cheap and useful for the final report; **no decision depends on it** since the weekly safeguard
was removed.

**No Week-1 slot is AI-treated**, so no AI production is required for launch — but the AI route must be proven
at the Sourcing Gate.

### 2.2 Layer 2 — the C1 production shell, required before Day 1 for every C1 slot

Everything except the subject and the finished creative:

- slot assignment frozen (already fixed by the matrix);
- the C1 template complete, with the specific format variant built;
- **asset route verified** for that slot's format;
- research and source checklist ready — which sources will verify price, date, sizing and name claims;
- subject-selection queue live, watchlist covering that slot's eligible window;
- **production capacity reserved** — a named, calendared block sized to the declared per-format time budget,
  sitting between the topic-lock and scheduling deadlines;
- the failure path predeclared per `EXCEPTION_PROTOCOL.md`.

### 2.3 C1 deadline ladder — identical for all 21 C1 slots, all six weeks

Measured from each slot's 16:00 UTC publish instant:

| Deadline | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **T − 48 h** | **TOPIC LOCK.** Subject drawn from the queue by the queue rule and logged with timestamp, item, rule applied and queue position. **Irreversible.** |
| **T − 24 h** | **PRODUCTION COMPLETE.** Creative finished, every factual claim verified against the logged sources, QA'd against the content-quality minimum. |
| **T − 12 h** | **SCHEDULED.** Post scheduled in-platform at its exact instant. From here the slot is treated exactly as a Layer-1 slot. |

**The ladder is uniform across all six weeks by design.** A Week-1-only special rule would make Week 1's C1
pieces systematically different in production maturity from later C1 pieces — an uncontrolled variable
correlated with week, inside the engine most exposed to it.

## 3. Production feasibility — recorded, not re-argued

Estimated combined load: **≈23 hours/week across both founders** (≈11.5 h each) — roughly 20 h/week of feed
production, research, sourcing, scheduling and measurement, plus ≈3 h/week for 84 Stories. These are estimates
with a wide band, not measurements; replace them with measured times after Week 1.

**This is inconsistent with the 10%-involvement aspiration for the duration of the test.** That tension is
recorded, not re-argued: this is a six-week sprint, not the operating model. The founders' cadence and Story
decisions are final.

Bottlenecks, ranked: **C2 image rights at volume** → 48 Reels from a team with no original-Reel production
history, on an account (B) that has never published a Reel → C3 research and fact-checking volume → 42
consecutive publish days including weekends → template build, which must precede all production.

Mitigations that change no frozen assignment: the matrix fixes the **publication** weekday, not the production
day — weekend posts are produced by Thursday and scheduled. Batch by format across engines (this also
de-biases effort across engines). Maintain the ≥7-piece buffer. Keep the 7-piece evergreen reserve stocked.

## 4. Content-quality minimum

Pre-declared so it cannot become an excuse to skip a hard slot:

original creative — no third-party file, no watermark, no reposted template · rights-clean imagery with source
and permission basis logged · **every factual claim (price, date, name, figure) verified against a named source
recorded in the tracking row** · correct house template applied · Reel 20–59 s, target 30–45 s, never over
90 s · carousel 5–10 slides · no engagement bait · caption free of the excluded advertiser categories · for AI
slots, the full `AI_IMAGERY_SUBTEST.md` §5 checklist plus disclosure.

**If a slot cannot meet this minimum in time, publish nothing.** Treat it as a Miss. A substandard piece
contaminates its engine's median more than a missing row does.
