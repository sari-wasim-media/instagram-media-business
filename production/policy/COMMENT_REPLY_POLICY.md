# Comment-reply policy — one declared policy, applied identically on both accounts

**Status: PROPOSED — founder confirmation required.** Comment-reply policy is a frozen variable
(`LOCK_SPECIFICATION.md` §1 item 16–17) that must be declared before Post #1. Consistency matters more than
the level: the same policy, the same effort, both accounts, all six weeks.

## The policy

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Window** | Replies happen within **24 hours** of each post's 16:00 UTC publish instant — in practice at the next day's production block. No replies after 72 hours (a late reply is an unmeasured re-engagement variable) |
| **Who gets a reply** | Every **substantive** comment: a question, a correction, a disagreement, a request for a source. Up to **15 replies per post**; beyond that, like only |
| **Likes** | Like every non-spam comment in the same window |
| **Voice** | The desk's: short, factual, sentence case, no emoji-only replies, no sarcasm at a commenter, no founder names, never "we/I" as a person — "the desk" or plain statements |
| **Corrections** | If a commenter is right, say so in the reply, fix nothing on the post (editing a live post is an unmeasured change), and log it for Thursday's DESK NOTES Story |
| **Never** | Ask for follows, saves, shares or tags · reply with a link · reply to identify a founder · pin any comment (pinning is a variable) · reply to a comment on another account's post · argue about counterfeits or price a "rep" |
| **Spam and scams** | Hide (do not delete) bot, replica-seller, growth-service and crypto comments; report where the platform offers it; note the volume in the account-weekly row if it exceeds 10 on any post |
| **Comments off?** | Never. Comments stay on for all 84 posts and all Stories' replies stay open |
| **DMs** | Answer business enquiries per the bio ("DM for business") with a holding reply and a log entry; **no advertiser conversation, quote or outreach during the experiment** (locked). No DM automation |
| **Breakout posts** | Same policy; the cap of 15 replies holds. Supporting a breakout happens inside that day's single Story, per the locked rule |

## Why 15

At 14 posts a week and a 24-hour window the cap keeps the load bounded (≤ 30 minutes per day for both
accounts), keeps effort identical across engines, and stops a breakout post from receiving an unmeasured
extra push from the desk. If the cap is hit on more than three posts in a week, note it at the weekly
review; do not raise it mid-test.
