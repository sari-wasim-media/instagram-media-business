# C3 data-source terms register

**Sourcing Gate item 6:** *per source, confirm manual collection and republication of derived figures is
permitted; sources recorded; no scraper during the experiment.*

**Status: OPEN — founder verification required per source before first use.** The research column records
what was read on 2026-09-05 (`SOURCING_ROUTES_RESEARCH.md` §2). The verification columns are the founders'
to fill. **A source is usable only when its row reads CLEARED with a date and initials.**

⚠ What the research found changes C3's default: several marketplaces' terms restrict copying of their
figures *by any means, including manual*, and restrict use to personal, non-commercial purposes. "Manual
collection from public marketplace pages" is therefore not automatically clean. C3's default sources are
**published reports and brand release notes**; marketplaces join the list only after a founder has read the
current terms and recorded the conclusion below.

## How to fill a row

1. Open the source's current terms page; save it as PDF with the date into the local asset store.
2. Find the clauses on (a) automated collection, (b) copying/reproducing/republishing content, (c) permitted
   purposes (personal / non-commercial).
3. Write the conclusion in one sentence: "Manual reading of public prices and republication of a derived
   median is / is not permitted because …". If in doubt, it is not permitted.
4. Date and initial the row. Re-read at the mid-test checkpoint.

## Operating rules that follow

- **No scraper, no extension, no export, no bulk download, ever** during the experiment (locked).
- **Facts are still facts:** a price is not copyrightable; the question with rows 3–11 is contractual
  (terms of use), not copyright. That is a founder-level legal judgement; record it once here, do not
  re-litigate it per piece.
- Every C3 number carries its source class and date on the frame and in the tracking row.
- Sample honesty: state size band, window and n; do not publish a median from fewer than 5 sales.
- If a row is NOT CLEARED, C3 pieces are written toward rows 1–2 (reports, release notes) and any CLEARED
  rows. That is enough for MOVE (report series), SPREAD (release-note retail vs report resale) and SHARE
  (report data). It is thinner for BELOW RETAIL, which needs live resale figures — plan those pieces only
  once a marketplace row is CLEARED.
