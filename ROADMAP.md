# Roadmap

High level only. **No dates** — the project has not committed to a calendar, and inventing one would be
fabrication.

The GitHub migration is infrastructure. It is not the business.

---

  map, automation ceiling, risk register
- **Account evidence collection** — Account Status, demographics, geography, 90-day passive performance,
  historical post samples, profile and grid baselines, for both accounts

- **Week 0 security** — 2FA enabled on both accounts
- **Week 0 legacy cleanup** — three UAPLG/counterfeit promotions documented and removed; Highlights verified
  clean; both accounts still green
- **Week 0 profile edit** — a deliberately minimal change: the follower-goal line removed from each bio, the
  rest kept substantially unchanged, then frozen. The proposed full bio rewrite was **not** applied
- **Repository migration** — this repository became the canonical source of truth
- **Workflow V1** — the canonical working workflow codified in `WORKFLOW.md`: founder roles, Claude's
  execution autonomy, the escalation rule, the branch-per-task Git flow and its finalization gate
- **The balanced 84-post matrix — designed, validated and FOUNDER-LOCKED.** 42 posts/account, 84 total, six
  weeks, 4R/2C/1S per account per week, weeks 3–6 balanced with zero residual, one fixed 16:00 UTC publish
  instant, a six-slot C4-only AI-imagery sub-test, and daily Stories
  → `experiment/active-84-post-plan/`

## Next

All operational. In rough order:

1. **Build the four house templates** — chart, ranking carousel, verdict card, meme layout — and **fix the C2
   ranked-item count**. Everything else depends on the per-piece asset counts these produce
2. **Resolve imagery sourcing** — owned, licensed, permissioned or self-generated. C2 is the constraint;
   C3's constraint is research time and data-source ToS, not licensing
3. **Build the tracking sheet** by importing `experiment/active-84-post-plan/matrix_84.csv`
4. **Build the topic backlog** and start the blind-to-performance check-out queue
5. **Declare the 7-day Story rotation**, fix the sticker weekday, write the fixed AI disclosure wording
6. **Produce and schedule** the 11 non-C1 Week-1 feed pieces, the 14 Week-1 Stories and the 7-piece reserve;
   execute the Day-2 C1 topic lock; complete Week-2 outlines
7. **Instantiate the start date** — the first Monday after both gates pass
8. **Launch** — only after 1–7 are complete

*Optional at any point, not blocking:* capture post-change profile screenshots, which would convert the
founder-confirmed bio edit into screenshot evidence. This is **not** a task to change the bios.

⚠ **The two gates bind.** If the Week-1 pieces are not produced and scheduled before Week 1 begins, **delay
launch** — do not start and slip. A test that collapses mid-run produces a false negative on assets that may
not be testable twice. Do **not** respond to a failed gate by altering the locked matrix.

## Later

Everything here is **conditional on the experiment's outcome** and must not be planned in detail before it.

- **Read the results** at the decision point — on medians of weeks 3–6, using Reach as the denominator, with
  outliers as ceiling evidence only, and a declared engine win requiring ≥2× on both accounts
- **Read the AI-imagery sub-test separately** — exploratory and directional, not powered; plus the mandatory
  sensitivity analysis and flip rule on C4
- **Decide which accounts and which content engines carry forward** — including whether any cell dominates on
  *both* accounts, which is the only way an engine effect can be claimed
- **Revisit the `sneakerbong` rename** — deferred until revival evidence exists
- **Revisit voiceover and link-in-bio** — both were restricted for the test only, not banned as a business
- **Choose a monetization model** — direct sponsorship, a productised content service, an owned-audience
  layer, or a hybrid. **Nothing is chosen.** The founders' underlying question — a media asset, or a business
  that reaches $6,000/month — is deliberately still open
- **Price discovery** — only real buyer conversations can price this; no validated CPM exists publicly for a
  faceless page at this scale
- **Settle governance** — legal entity, written partnership terms, a deadlock mechanism for the 50/50 split.
  Recommended before first revenue
- **Automation** — only after reliability is proven, and never for advertiser vetting, brand-safety judgement,
  pricing, negotiation, contract terms or account access
- **Network expansion** — every page needs its own content line; the cheap one-content-line-many-pages model
  does not work

---

## What this roadmap deliberately does not contain

No revenue forecasts, no follower targets treated as plans, no launch date, and no monetization commitment.
The $6,000/month figure is a **goal**; the 500K-follower figure is a **de-emphasised aspiration** that later
research judged generous. Neither is a schedule.
