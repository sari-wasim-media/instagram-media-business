# Content Engines

**Status: the four engines are FOUNDER-LOCKED. No Week-1 topics, no content and no backlog has been designed.**

These are the four faceless content engines carried unchanged into the locked 84-post matrix. Each receives
**exactly 21 posts pooled — 12 Reels, 6 Carousels, 3 Statics** — see `../active-84-post-plan/MATRIX_84.md`.
Adding or dropping an engine is prohibited for the duration of the controlled test.

---

## C1 — Release & Verdict Desk

Original graphics and short Reels reacting to actual releases within 24–48 hours of news breaking: price and
sizing breakdowns in the house layout, ranked "this week's drops", "worth it / wait for the sale" verdicts.

Reel primary, static secondary, carousel occasional. Distribution thesis: timeliness plus keyword relevance;
opinionated verdicts drive comments. Production ~40 min/piece once templated, but requires daily
release-calendar monitoring. **Key metric:** non-follower reach share, plus comments per reach.

## C2 — Rankings & Tier Lists

Original ranked carousels in a fixed house layout — slide 1 hook, one item per slide, summary slide.

Carousel primary, Reel secondary. Distribution thesis: ranking content drives saves and repeat views.
Binding constraint is **imagery rights** — every asset must be owned, licensed or permissioned. **Key
metric:** saves per reach, plus profile visits per reach.

*Lineage note:* this is the original version of a format the accounts already proved they could execute — the
historical AJ1 colorway grid in the evidence pack was exactly this format, but reposted. Same format,
originated instead of taken.

## C3 — Market & Price Intelligence

Original charts and data cards in a consistent visual system — resale price movement, retail-vs-resale
spreads, brand share shifts, which releases trade below retail.

Static and carousel primary, animated-chart Reel secondary. Highest commercial relevance of the four and the
highest later automation potential — the only cell that becomes a data pipeline rather than a craft process.
Also the most research-heavy (~90 min/piece). **Key metric:** saves per reach, plus profile visits per reach.

**Data sourcing rule: manual collection from public marketplace pages and published reports. Check terms of
service before any automated collection. Do not build a scraper during the experiment.**

## C4 — Original Meme & Commentary

Originated formats using owned or licensed imagery and original writing.

**Hard rule: never a reposted meme template, never a screenshot of another account, never someone else's
watermarked image.**

Reel primary, static secondary. Distribution thesis: sends per reach — memes travel by DM. Lowest commercial
relevance and hardest to place a sponsor inside without damage; highest quality variance. **Key metric:**
sends per reach.

## Naming lineage

An earlier generation named these four cells *market intelligence · utility/comparison · original
meme/commentary · editorial analysis*. Those are the **Phase 2 ancestors** of C1–C4, not a competing set —
the cells were later redefined so each tests a distribution *mechanism* rather than a topic. If you encounter
the older naming, it is the earlier generation. See `../../strategy/historical/CORRECTIONS_LEDGER.md`.

## Faceless constraint

All four engines are faceless-compatible, which is why they were chosen. **Neither founder appears on camera,
in photographs, in video, or as the public personality behind either account.**

**On voiceover — DECIDED.** ⚠ **No voiceover during the six-week controlled test.** Founder-locked by Sari and
Wasim. All 48 Reels use a uniform non-voiceover production method: text-on-screen with licensed or
royalty-free audio. This is **test-specific, not a permanent business prohibition** — it holds production
method constant across the experiment rather than settling voiceover as a business question. See
`../../DECISIONS.md`.

## AI-generated imagery — permitted in C4 only, six slots

Founder-locked. AI-generated imagery appears in **exactly six of the 84 feed posts, all in C4**, three per
account, one Reel + one Carousel + one Static each, as a **logged nested sub-test**.

**Prohibited in C1, C2 and C3** — those engines cover real, specific products and factual claims, and
generated imagery there would misrepresent a real product. C4 is the only engine whose visuals are inherently
stylized and not factually load-bearing.

Full specification, including what qualifies as AI-generated imagery, the prohibited-use list, disclosure and
the analysis rule: **`../active-84-post-plan/AI_IMAGERY_SUBTEST.md`**. Using AI imagery in any other slot is a
protocol violation.

## Imagery rights — the binding operational constraint

⚠ **C2 is the constraint, and it is different in kind from C3.** C2's canonical format needs one rights-clean
image **per ranked item, per piece, in every format** — roughly 85–170 distinct images across its 21 posts.
C3's visuals are **self-generated charts**; its constraints are research time and data-source terms of
service, not image licensing. Older checklists bundle these as "C2 and C3" sourcing; they are not the same
problem. See `../active-84-post-plan/GATES.md` §1.
