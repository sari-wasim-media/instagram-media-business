# Project Brief

## Venture

Sari and Wasim are reviving two dormant Instagram accounts and intend to build them into a larger network of profitable niche media pages. The initial niche is sneakers and streetwear.

This is intended to become a serious long-term business and, over time, a highly automated end-to-end media operation.

## Founders

- Sari and Wasim are equal 50/50 partners.
- They share the same commercial goal and intend to make decisions jointly based on what is best for the business.
- Sari is currently the primary administrator of both Instagram accounts.
- Wasim also has access to both accounts.
- Their long-term operating roles have not yet been assigned.

## Existing accounts

### `thestreetwearofficial`

- Approximately 24,000 followers.
- Inactive for approximately two years.

### `sneakerbong`

- Approximately 20,800 followers.
- Last post was in 2021.
- Broadly similar audience to the first account.

### Current account condition

- Both accounts currently show clean Account Status.
- No known banned, deleted or removed posts.
- Both are managed directly through Instagram.
- They are not yet connected to Facebook Pages or a structured Meta Business Portfolio.
- Usernames, bios, profile images and brand identity may be changed if research supports doing so.

## Twelve-month ambition

- $6,000 in combined monthly revenue.
- 500,000 combined followers across the future page network.
- No more than 10% combined human involvement from Sari and Wasim.

These are targets to be tested and reverse-engineered, not assumptions Claude should automatically accept as feasible.

## Funding model

- Maximum initial private setup budget: $300.
- The initial budget is reserved for necessary tools, APIs, servers and infrastructure.
- After revenue begins, business growth should be funded from revenue.
- The founders do not intend to withdraw profits during the first 12 months.
- Revenue should be reinvested into content, growth, operations and infrastructure.

## Commercial direction

The preferred model is recurring, niche-aligned advertising rather than isolated one-off promotions.

Ideal customers include relevant sneaker or streetwear brands, suppliers, retailers, resale stores and e-commerce businesses. The intended outcome is for clients to buy recurring packages across multiple pages and renew when each campaign period ends.

The business is open to additional revenue streams if they do not damage audience trust.

## Growth direction

- Organic growth is preferred.
- Collaborations and giveaways are acceptable when their expected value can be measured.
- Expansion into additional niches is possible when supported by strong ROI and operational fit.
- Buying accounts must not be the foundation of the plan.
- Safer expansion paths such as launching pages, partnerships, management agreements and revenue-sharing should be evaluated.

## Historical operation

- Payments were historically received through PayPal.
- A formal invoicing, contract and record-keeping structure has not yet been established.
- Historical account-level Insights are currently limited because of inactivity.
- Some individual historical posts reportedly achieved approximately 300,000 to 1,000,000 impressions.
- The included content files and price menu are the main current historical evidence.

## Long-term E2E vision

The future operating system may eventually cover content discovery, suitability checks, content production, approvals, scheduling, publishing, community management, DMs, lead qualification, proposals, negotiation support, payments, campaign delivery, reporting, renewals, analytics and continuous learning.

Automation authority should increase gradually only after reliability is demonstrated.

## Current phase

The current phase is research, audit, strategic design and master planning with Claude Opus 5.

Claude Code and implementation are explicitly deferred until the strategy has been reviewed and approved.
