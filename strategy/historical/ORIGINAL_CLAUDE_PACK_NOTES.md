## What still holds

- **The founders' constraints.** Budget cap, reinvestment intent, content boundaries, excluded advertisers,
  organic-growth preference, and the instruction not to build the business on grey automation. These fed
  directly into `../business/BUSINESS_CONTEXT.md` and `../../DECISIONS.md`.
- **The targets, as targets.** $6,000/month and 500K followers were stated as ambitions to be reverse-
  engineered and stress-tested, never as assumptions. Later research treated them accordingly.
- **The format-literacy read.** The ten creatives demonstrate real competence at visual density,
  price-anchored comparison, and list/grid formats — transferable skills and among the strongest faceless
  formats available. This was later called a genuine, undervalued asset.
- **The authority limits.** The pack explicitly withheld authority to write code, involve Claude Code,
  publish or modify Instagram content, change account settings, contact clients, or begin irreversible
  execution. That posture still governs: this repository is documentation, and no business execution has been
  performed through Claude Code.

## What has since changed

- **"Both accounts have no known banned, deleted or removed posts."** True when written. Three UAPLG
  promotions have since been removed — **by founder decision, not by platform action.** Both accounts remain
  green.
- **"Not yet connected to Facebook Pages or a structured Meta Business Portfolio."** Stated then; not
  re-verified since. Treat as unverified.
- **"We are open to changing usernames, bios, profile images and brand identity."** Still true in principle,
  but the profile is now **frozen** for the duration of the controlled experiment, and the `sneakerbong`
  rename is explicitly deferred.
- **"Collaborations and giveaways are acceptable when the expected benefit is measurable."** Still the
  long-run position, but **prohibited during the controlled experiment**. Scope-limited, not reversed.
- **Account follower counts** have drifted down through dormancy — see `../../evidence/README.md` for the
  measured baseline.

## Caution

The pack predates every piece of real account data. Where it and the measured evidence disagree, **the
evidence wins**. Where it and a later decision disagree, **the later decision wins**.

Its ten creatives are examples of content language, **not** a performance sample — the pack says so itself.
Do not infer which formats worked from them.
