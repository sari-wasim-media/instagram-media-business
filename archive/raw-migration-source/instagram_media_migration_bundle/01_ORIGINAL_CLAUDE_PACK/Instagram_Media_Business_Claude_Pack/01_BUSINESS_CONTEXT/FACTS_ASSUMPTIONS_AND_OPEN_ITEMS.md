# Facts, Assumptions and Open Items

## Confirmed facts

- There are two existing Instagram accounts with approximately 44,800 combined followers.
- Both accounts have been inactive for years.
- Both currently show clean Account Status.
- Sari and Wasim are 50/50 partners.
- The private setup budget is capped at $300.
- First-year profits are intended to be reinvested.
- PayPal was historically used for payments.
- Organic growth is preferred.
- The founders want recurring niche-aligned advertisers.
- The accounts are not yet connected to a structured Meta Business setup.
- The founders are open to rebranding when justified.

## Working targets that require validation

- $6,000 monthly revenue by month 12.
- 500,000 combined followers by month 12.
- No more than 10% combined founder involvement.
- The ability to fund future expansion entirely from operating revenue.
- The demand and pricing potential of multi-page recurring advertising packages.

## Current evidence limitations

- No complete historical performance export has been provided yet.
- Current account-level Insights are nearly empty because of inactivity.
- Post-level Insights exist for at least some historical posts but are not included yet.
- Historical captions and posting dates are not included.
- Historical DM and client records may be limited.
- Past revenue and renewal rates have not yet been reconstructed.

These limitations should affect confidence levels, but they should not prevent the strategy process from beginning.

## Decisions intentionally left open

- Whether either account should be renamed or repositioned.
- Whether both accounts should publish similar content or have distinct roles.
- Exact founder roles.
- New content mix and publishing cadence.
- New pricing and packages.
- Whether and when to expand into other niches.
- Which tools and technical architecture should eventually be used.
- The exact point at which Claude Code should be involved.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Do not build or deploy software yet.
- Do not modify either Instagram account yet.
- Do not contact customers or publish content yet.
- Do not design the core operation around risky grey automation.
- Do not recommend unrelated advertising that damages audience trust.
- Do not recommend sexual, political or drug-related content.
- Do not treat account acquisition as the core growth strategy.
