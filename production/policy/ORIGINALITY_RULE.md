# The originality rule — the one page both founders keep open

*(Week-0 checklist item 26: "write the originality rule where both founders can see it." This is it.)*

# ZERO unoriginal or reposted feed posts during the controlled experiment.

Not "mostly original". Not "one won't hurt". **Zero**, on both accounts, for all 84 posts, all 84 Stories,
and every reserve piece.

## What counts as original here

- Made by us: our photograph, our chart, our illustration, our layout, our writing.
- Or **licensed / permissioned** imagery, materially transformed inside our own layout, with the licence or
  permission archived and a provenance ID on file.
- Or, in exactly six C4 slots, disclosed AI illustration under the sub-test rules.

## What is never posted

- Another account's post, Reel, Story or screenshot — with or without credit, with or without "materially
  enhancing" it.
- A meme template, a viral clip, a "found" image, a watermark-cropped picture, a brand's post reshared.
- A repost of our own earlier post as a new post.
- A file that also exists on the other account (never the same file on both).

## Why zero, in five lines

1. Attribution: a repost measures someone else's content, not our system.
2. A suppressed post wastes a matrix slot and returns no measurement.
3. The experiment is partly a test of whether two people can produce original content at cadence.
4. It removes interpretive ambiguity.
5. Zero against a platform threshold of ten is a margin of safety.

**Not** because one repost makes an account ineligible — it does not. The platform acts on accounts that
*repeatedly* post unoriginal content (the help page still says "fewer than 10 … over the last 30 days"; the
April 2026 extension to photos and carousels speaks of accounts that "primarily" post unoriginal content and
notes that borders, watermarks, subtitles and caption credits do not make a post original). Our rule is
stricter than the platform's on purpose. See `strategy/historical/CORRECTIONS_LEDGER.md`, correction 1.

## If it happens anyway

It is a **protocol violation, not an emergency.** Log it. Do not panic-delete. Exclude the observation.
Replace the slot preserving account, engine, format, weekday and instant, extending the calendar if needed.
Do not pause the account. No clock resets. Three on one account inside 30 days → production-process review.
(`EXCEPTION_PROTOCOL.md`.)

## The test before every publish

> Could someone show me where every pixel and every word in this post came from, and would every answer be
> "us", "licensed — archived", "permitted — archived" or "AI, in one of the six slots, disclosed"?

If any answer is "somewhere online", it does not go out.
