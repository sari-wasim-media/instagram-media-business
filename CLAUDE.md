the next task, and git safety rules.

1. **The 48-post matrix in `experiment/historical-48-post-design/` is SUPERSEDED.** It is detailed and looks
   authoritative. It was replaced before launch by a 1-post/day/account cadence (84 planned posts). The
   84-post matrix does not exist yet.
2. **Check `strategy/historical/CORRECTIONS_LEDGER.md` before citing any historical document.** They correct
   each other across five generations; several confident claims in the earlier ones were withdrawn.
3. **This project documents and asks — it does not execute.** No publishing, no automation, no monetization,
   no pushing to `origin` without explicit founder approval.
