# Optional Follow-Up Evidence

The strategy session should begin now. The following materials may be added later when available:

- Post-level Insights for 15–25 high-performing posts.
- Insights for 10–15 ordinary posts as a comparison group.
- Captions and posting dates.
- Audience geography, age and gender data.
- Historical DMs with personal information removed.
- Historical PayPal transaction summaries with sensitive information removed.
- Evidence of repeat customers or campaign renewals.
- A full Instagram data export from each account.
- Current screenshots of profile, bio, feed and Account Status.

When new evidence arrives, Claude should update assumptions and recommendations rather than restarting the entire strategy process.
