## Commercial model

We do not want to depend on one-off promotions.

The ideal customer is a niche-relevant business that repeatedly renews advertising packages—for example:

- A sneaker supplier or retailer.
- A streetwear brand.
- A resale store.
- A relevant e-commerce business.
- Another legitimate business aligned with the audience.

An example could be a repeating package containing posts and stories across several pages. When one campaign period ends, the goal is for the client to renew immediately.

We want to explore:

- Recurring advertising packages.
- Network-wide packages across multiple pages.
- Retainers or subscription-like arrangements.
- Upsells and longer commitments.
- Performance reporting that demonstrates value.
- A client-retention system that makes renewals natural.
- Additional revenue streams that do not damage audience trust.

We do not want unrelated advertisers simply because they are willing to pay. Losing audience trust for short-term income would harm the asset.
