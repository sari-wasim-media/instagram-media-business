### The outlier

`2023-10-29_reel_wtf-mom-2-6m-views-outlier.jpeg` — Reel, 6 seconds, **2.6M views · 128K likes · 3.2K comments
· 6.5K saves**.

This is **ceiling / breakout evidence only.** It predates the current originality enforcement regime and says
nothing about distribution available today. Its value is as a prior about content–audience fit: when this
content did reach people, they responded strongly. It is not a target and must never be used as a baseline.

⚠ **Treat every historical number here as archaeology.** These posts ran at engagement rates far above current
platform norms, on a platform that distributed aggregator content very differently. **Not one of them is a
target.**

### Originality note

Several samples are visibly unoriginal — one still carries a TikTok watermark (`@blakelinder7`), others are
Simpsons/SpongeBob meme templates and third-party photographs. This is direct evidence for the
"historical repost-heavy operating model" that the revival experiment deliberately abandons.

does not exist, since originality is assessed on a rolling recent window.

### What these screenshots do and do not evidence

**They evidence the surrounding profile state**, which the founders have confirmed was kept substantially
unchanged. The only material edit made afterwards was the **removal of the follower-goal line** from each
bio — `🎯 | 30,000 follower` on Account A, `Goal: 40,000 followers 🎯` on Account B. Both lines are readable
in these screenshots, so what was removed is documented even though the post-removal state is not.

**That removal is founder-confirmed but not separately screenshot-verified.** Everything else visible here is
current: the founders have confirmed that **the display names were not changed either** — "streetwear life"
and "Sneakerbong" both stand — along with the remaining bio lines, the cross-promotion line on `sneakerbong`,
highlights, and follower and post counts.

⚠ **An earlier project draft proposed replacing both bios entirely. That rewrite was never applied.** Do not
read these as "old bios awaiting replacement" — apart from the removed goal line, they are the current state.

A post-change capture would upgrade the removal from founder-confirmed to screenshot-evidenced. It is an
**optional evidence improvement, not a blocker**. See `../PROJECT_STATE.md`.
