⚠ **The two gates bind.** If the Week-1 pieces are not produced and scheduled before Week 1 begins, **delay
launch** — do not start and slip. A test that collapses mid-run produces a false negative on assets that may
not be testable twice. Do **not** respond to a failed gate by altering the locked matrix.

## Later

Everything here is **conditional on the experiment's outcome** and must not be planned in detail before it.

- **Read the results** at the decision point — on medians of weeks 3–6, using Reach as the denominator, with
  outliers as ceiling evidence only, and a declared engine win requiring ≥2× on both accounts
- **Read the AI-imagery sub-test separately** — exploratory and directional, not powered; plus the mandatory
  sensitivity analysis and flip rule on C4
- **Decide which accounts and which content engines carry forward** — including whether any cell dominates on
  *both* accounts, which is the only way an engine effect can be claimed
- **Revisit the `sneakerbong` rename** — deferred until revival evidence exists
- **Revisit voiceover and link-in-bio** — both were restricted for the test only, not banned as a business
- **Choose a monetization model** — direct sponsorship, a productised content service, an owned-audience
  layer, or a hybrid. **Nothing is chosen.** The founders' underlying question — a media asset, or a business
  that reaches $6,000/month — is deliberately still open
- **Price discovery** — only real buyer conversations can price this; no validated CPM exists publicly for a
  faceless page at this scale
- **Settle governance** — legal entity, written partnership terms, a deadlock mechanism for the 50/50 split.
  Recommended before first revenue
- **Automation** — only after reliability is proven, and never for advertiser vetting, brand-safety judgement,
  pricing, negotiation, contract terms or account access
- **Network expansion** — every page needs its own content line; the cheap one-content-line-many-pages model
  does not work

---

## What this roadmap deliberately does not contain

No revenue forecasts, no follower targets treated as plans, no launch date, and no monetization commitment.
The $6,000/month figure is a **goal**; the 500K-follower figure is a **de-emphasised aspiration** that later
research judged generous. Neither is a schedule.
